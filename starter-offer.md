# Starter Offer — Local Business Landing Page

## Who this is for

This offer is for a small local service business that needs a simple, mobile-friendly website customers can understand quickly.

Examples:

* Mobile detailers
* Cleaners
* Landscapers
* Barbers
* Tutors
* Handymen
* Small contractors
* Local service providers

## The problem I solve

Many small businesses have no website, an outdated website, or a page that does not clearly explain services, service area, hours, trust signals, and contact options.

That can make it harder for potential customers to know what the business does and how to request a quote.

## My starter package

I will create a simple one-page landing page that includes:

* Clear headline and service description
* Mobile-friendly layout
* Service sections
* Trust-building section
* Service-area section
* FAQ section
* Quote request/contact section
* Tap-to-call link
* Basic SEO title and meta description
* Public live link
* Basic page-quality test

## Beginner price

$250–$400 for the first few clients.

## Timeline

3–5 days for a simple one-page site after receiving the business details.

* Business name

## How I would explain it to a client

I build simple, mobile-friendly landing pages for local businesses so potential customers can quickly understand what you offer, where you work, and how to contact you. My focus is clarity, trust, and making it easier for someone to request a quote.
